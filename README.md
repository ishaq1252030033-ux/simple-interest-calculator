# Simple Interest Calculator

This project is a simple interest calculator that
calculates the interest on a principal amount based
on the annual rate of interest and time period.

## Description

A calculator that calculates simple interest given
the principal amount, annual rate of interest,
and time period in years.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:
- P = Principal amount
- R = Annual rate of interest
- T = Time period in years

## Example

Principal = 1000
Rate = 5%
Time = 2 years

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100
Simple Interest = 100
